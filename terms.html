Smart Punctuation is a Google Docs add-on that edits punctuation in the document you are actively working on, converting plain characters (straight quotes, apostrophes, double hyphens) into typographic equivalents (curly quotes, em dashes, en dashes) according to rules you choose to enable.

Use at your own discretion

This add-on directly modifies the text of your Google Doc when you run it. While it is designed to make only punctuation substitutions and to preserve existing text formatting, you are responsible for reviewing the result after running it. Google Docs' built-in Undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) reverts the entirety of a single run of the add-on in one step.
