Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Smart Punctuation is a Google Docs add-on that converts plain punctuation (straight quotes, apostrophes, double hyphens) into typographically correct characters (curly quotes, em dashes, en dashes) within a Google Doc.

What the add-on accesses

The add-on requests access to the Google Doc you are actively editing at the time you run it, and to Google's built-in menu and sidebar interface for that document. It does not access any other documents in your Google Drive, your email, your calendar, or any other Google service.

What the add-on does with document content

When you run the add-on, it reads the text of the current selection or the current document, applies punctuation substitutions, and writes the result back into that same document. This processing happens entirely within Google's Apps Script environment. The add-on does not transmit, upload, log, or store the content of your document anywhere. No document text is sent to any external server, third party, or service outside of Google's own infrastructure.

What the add-on stores

The add-on saves a small set of your preferences — which punctuation rules you have enabled or disabled, and your chosen sidebar display size — using Google Apps Script's built-in user property storage. This storage is private to your own Google account and is not accessible to the developer, to other users, or to any third party. No document content is included in this stored data.

Data sharing

This add-on does not share any data with third parties. It has no external server, database, or analytics integration of any kind.
